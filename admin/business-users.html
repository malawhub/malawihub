💼 Business Hub Users

This is the administrator side. Register users who already have a MalawiHub account. They will use the separate Business Hub to view their own wallet messages and balances and link Airtel Money/TNM Mpamba phone numbers. They cannot edit business records.

1. Create business workspace

2. Register a MalawiHub user

The email must already exist in MalawiHub. This does not create a second administrator account.

Access model

  • Administrator: registers Business Hub users from this Admin area.
  • Business user: logs into /business/.
  • Wallet linking: user links their own Airtel Money and/or TNM Mpamba number.
  • Read-only: user can see wallet messages and balances but cannot edit transactions, notifications, integrations or business settings.
