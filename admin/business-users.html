💼 MalawiHub Business Users

Manage Business users only. Business accounts remain separate from Main Hub and Online Class accounts.

1. Business workspace

2. Register or resend Business access

If the email already belongs to this Business account, a fresh 8-character access code and the existing username will be sent again. Main Hub and Online Class accounts are not converted.

The user receives the username, 8-character access code and Business login link by email.

3. Unregister Business user

Use this when a Business user should be completely removed. The Business membership and its Business-only Auth identity are removed so the email can be registered again as a new Business user.

Important: Only use this for Business users. The system will refuse to delete an account that is not marked as Business-only.
