💼 Business Hub Users

The administrator registers existing MalawiHub accounts as either employees or authorized employers. Everyone signs in through the same user Business Hub link. Access is read-only and enforced by Supabase RLS.

1. Business workspace

2. Register user

The email must already exist in MalawiHub. Platform administrators cannot be registered as Business users.

Access model

  • Employee: sees only their own linked wallet messages and balances.
  • Employer: sees authorized employees in their business, their wallet links, available balances and wallet messages.
  • Neither role: can edit transactions, balances, messages, integrations or business settings from the user portal.

User link: https://malawihub.pages.dev/business/
