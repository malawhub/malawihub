Find Educational Resources

Find open-access educational and academic materials, classify them by subject and topic, check duplicates, and add approved resources to the MalawiHub library.

Discovery currently uses OpenAlex's open-access scholarly catalog. Results are checked for an available open-access location and PDF where possible. A free-to-read resource is not automatically safe to redistribute. Resources without a clear reuse license are marked Needs Review rather than being automatically published.
