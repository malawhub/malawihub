MALAWI STUDENT LEARNING PLATFORM

Study resources and student tools for Malawi

MalawiHub is designed to make studying easier for learners in Malawi. Students can find revision materials, subject resources, past papers and practical study tools without searching through many separate websites. Use the GPA calculator to work out your grade point average, use the grade and percentage calculators for academic work, build a study timetable, or explore Chemistry and other subject resources.

MalawiHub also supports online learning through live classes, teacher notes and video resources. The platform is built to be simple and mobile-friendly so students can access useful learning materials from a phone.
