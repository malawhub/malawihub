What is GPA?

GPA is a summary of your academic performance across courses. The exact grading scale can differ between institutions, so use the scale that applies to your programme.

How to use the calculator

Enter your course information and grades, check the result and keep the result for your own study planning. For official academic decisions, confirm the grading rules used by your institution.
