👨‍🏫 MalawiHub Teacher Portal

Private portal for registered MalawiHub teachers. It is completely separate from the Student Portal, Main Hub and Business Hub.

Teachers can register themselves with their own email and password. Confirm your email if MalawiHub asks you to, then return here and your Teacher Dashboard will open automatically.

🌱 Start with just one class

You do not need to move your whole school to MalawiHub. Try one subject or one class first, then share the experience with your students.

🚀 How MalawiHub works for teachers

1. Get your teacher account
Create your teacher account with your own email and password. Confirm your email if requested.
2. Create your class
After signing in, create your subject/class and MalawiHub generates a class code.
3. Share one link
Share the class link with students through WhatsApp or another channel.
4. Students join
Students open the link, log in and join your class.
👩‍🏫 New teacher? Create your own account below and start your first class. You can then share one class link with your students.
