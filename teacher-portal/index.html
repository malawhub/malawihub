👨‍🏫 MalawiHub Teacher Portal

Private portal for registered MalawiHub teachers. It is completely separate from the Student Portal, Main Hub and Business Hub.

Teachers can create their own Online Class account. The administrator does not need to register each teacher before they can start a class.

🌱 Start with just one class

You do not need to move your whole school to MalawiHub. Try one subject or one class first, then share the experience with your students.

🚀 How MalawiHub works for teachers

1. Get your teacher account
The MalawiHub administrator creates your teacher account and provides your login details.
2. Create your class
After logging in, create your subject/class and MalawiHub generates a class code.
3. Share one link
Share the class link with students through WhatsApp or another channel.
4. Students join
Students open the link, log in and join your class.
👩‍🏫 New teacher? Create your own account and start your first class. You can then share one class link with your students.

Forgot password?

This permanently removes your Online Class teacher account.
