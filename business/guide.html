💼 MALZORA Business Guide

Detailed operating guide for MalawiHub/MALZORA Business administrators, employers and employees.

1. How MALZORA Business works

MALZORA Business uses one user entry point for registered employees and employers. The administrator creates the Business account first. The registered user's username and temporary password are sent to the user's real email address. The user then signs in, changes the temporary password, and is taken to the correct dashboard based on their Business role.

ADMIN

Administrator

Creates businesses, registers Business users, assigns employee/employer roles, and manages the wider platform.

EMPLOYER

Employer

Uses the same Business login link to view the business, employees, authorized wallet information and wallet messages available to the employer.

EMPLOYEE

Employee / Agent

Uses the same Business login link to view their own Business information and link their own Airtel Money and/or TNM Mpamba agent wallet number.

2. Administrator: complete workflow

  1. Open the administrator portal and sign in with the administrator account.
  2. Open MALZORA Business Users.
  3. Create the Business workspace by entering the business name and a unique link/slug name.
  4. Select the newly created Business workspace.
  5. Enter the user's full legal/display name.
  6. Enter the user's real email address. This is important because the credentials are delivered there.
  7. Create a unique username for the Business login.
  8. Select the role: Employee / agent or Employer / read-only oversight.
  9. Press Register and email credentials.
  10. The system generates a secure temporary password. The administrator does not see or manually choose that password.
  11. The user receives an email containing the Business login link, username and temporary password.
  12. Tell the user to check Inbox, Spam/Junk and Promotions if the email is not immediately visible.
  13. The user signs in and must create a new private password on the first login.
Security rule: Administrators should never ask users to send their passwords through WhatsApp or display passwords publicly. The temporary password is intended for the registered user's email only.

3. Administrator: registering an employee

  1. Create/select the correct Business workspace.
  2. Enter the employee's name and real email.
  3. Choose a username that is easy for the employee to remember but is not already used.
  4. Select Employee / agent.
  5. Register the account.
  6. The employee receives credentials by email.
  7. After first login, the employee changes the temporary password.
  8. The employee can then link their own Airtel Money and/or TNM Mpamba agent wallet number.

An employee should only link a wallet number that belongs to them and that they are authorized to use.

4. Administrator: registering an employer

  1. Select the correct Business workspace.
  2. Enter the employer's name, real email and unique username.
  3. Select Employer / read-only oversight.
  4. Register and email the credentials.
  5. The employer signs in through the same Business user link.
  6. The system identifies the employer role and opens the employer dashboard.

The employer does not need a separate registration link.

5. Business user: first login

  1. Open https://malawihub.pages.dev/business/users/.
  2. Enter the username from the registration email.
  3. Enter the temporary password from the same email.
  4. Press Sign in.
  5. On the first login, create a new private password.
  6. After the password is changed, the system opens the correct dashboard automatically.

Employees and employers use the same login page. The Business role determines which dashboard they receive.

6. Employee / agent: daily work

  1. Sign in through the Business user link.
  2. Confirm that the displayed Business name and your name are correct.
  3. Open Link your agent wallets.
  4. For Airtel Money, enter your authorized agent phone number and press Link Airtel Money.
  5. For TNM Mpamba, enter your authorized agent phone number and press Link TNM Mpamba.
  6. Wait for the provider connection/synchronization process. A newly linked wallet can initially show Pending connection.
  7. When a provider feed is available and connected, your authorized wallet balance can appear in the wallet section.
  8. Wallet messages received through an authorized provider feed can appear under Wallet messages.
  9. Use Sign out when you finish on a shared or public device.
Important: Linking a phone number by itself does not give MALZORA legal or technical access to private Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba data. Live balances/messages require an authorized provider feed/API connection.

7. Employer: daily work

  1. Open the same Business user link.
  2. Sign in with the employer username and password.
  3. The system identifies the employer role automatically.
  4. Review the Business name and employee list.
  5. Review wallet balance records that are authorized for employer viewing.
  6. Review wallet messages available to the employer.
  7. Use the information for business oversight and reconciliation.
  8. Sign out after finishing.

The employer dashboard is intended to be read-only. Employees, transactions, balances and provider data should not be manually edited from the user-facing dashboard.

8. Malipo: what it is used for

Malipo is the payment-processing part of the Business system. It is different from the direct Airtel Money/TNM Mpamba wallet feed.

Payment request

MALZORA can create a payment request through the Malipo integration for a supported provider.

Transaction status

A payment can be tracked using its merchant transaction reference and Malipo transaction information.

Callback

Malipo can notify the MALZORA backend when a supported transaction reaches a final status.

Reconciliation

Administrators/employers can use confirmed Malipo records to reconcile business payments.

Do not confuse them: Malipo payment transactions are not the same thing as an employee's private agent-wallet balance or SMS/message feed.

9. Handling a Malipo payment

  1. Start the payment from the authorized Business payment workflow.
  2. Select the supported provider, such as Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba.
  3. Enter the required customer/payment details and amount.
  4. Submit the payment request.
  5. Keep the generated merchant transaction reference.
  6. Check the transaction status through the Malipo enquiry/callback workflow.
  7. If the status is Incomplete or another non-final state, do not manually mark it as completed.
  8. Only a confirmed final successful status should be treated as completed by the system.
  9. If money appears to have been deducted but the payment is still incomplete, keep the transaction reference and investigate through the official provider/Malipo support process rather than editing the database manually.

10. What administrators should NOT do

  • Do not share secret API keys in the browser, GitHub files or WhatsApp.
  • Do not manually change a user's password in the database.
  • Do not manually mark an incomplete Malipo payment as completed.
  • Do not invent wallet balances or transaction records.
  • Do not ask employees for private wallet passwords/PINs.
  • Do not register a person using someone else's email address without authorization.
  • Do not give employees administrator credentials.

11. What users should NOT do

  • Do not share your Business username and password with another person.
  • Do not share your Airtel Money/TNM Mpamba PIN with MALZORA administrators.
  • Do not link another person's wallet number to your account.
  • Do not use a temporary password after you have completed the first-login password change.
  • Do not leave your Business account signed in on a shared phone.
  • Report suspicious access or an incorrect Business assignment to the administrator.

12. If something does not work

  • Credentials email not received: check Spam/Junk/Promotions and confirm the administrator used the correct email address. Production email delivery also requires a configured email service.
  • Username/password rejected: confirm that the username is exactly the one in the email and that the temporary password has not already been replaced.
  • Password change fails: make sure the new password meets the minimum requirements and both password fields match.
  • Business access denied: ask the administrator to confirm that your account is registered as an employee or employer.
  • Wallet says Pending connection: the wallet link has been recorded, but the authorized provider feed has not yet supplied live data.
  • Malipo says Incomplete: do not manually edit it. Keep the merchant transaction reference and use the Malipo enquiry/support workflow.

13. If you are both Admin and Business user

You can have both platform administrator access and a Business membership. Use the correct entry point for the task:

Platform work

Use the Admin Hub for user registration, platform administration and Business management.

Business work

Use Business User Access to work as your registered employee/employer role.

This keeps administrator functions separate from normal Business operations while allowing the same person to hold both authorized roles.

14. Quick daily checklist

  • ☐ Sign in using your own credentials.
  • ☐ Confirm the correct Business and role.
  • ☐ Check wallet/provider status where applicable.
  • ☐ Review Malipo payments through official transaction status.
  • ☐ Never manually fake a balance or completed payment.
  • ☐ Protect passwords and wallet PINs.
  • ☐ Sign out when using a shared device.
