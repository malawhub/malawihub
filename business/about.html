What can it help your business do?

🔐 A private business workspace

Each business has its own dashboard and business records are kept separate from other businesses.

📲 Connect a business phone

Pair the Android phone that receives your supported Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba transaction messages.

💰 View wallet information

See reported wallet balances and the supported transaction messages received by the connected phone.

👥 Employer and employee access

Authorized employers can oversee their own business employees and view the wallet information available to their workspace.

Who can use it?

It is intended for transport operators and other businesses that receive supported Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba payments. Each business registers its own workspace. The dashboard shows information received from the connected phone; it does not move money or replace Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba services.

How the free trial works

  1. Register your business with an email address and create your private workspace.
  2. Verify your email and sign in to your Business Hub dashboard.
  3. Install the Business Connector APK on the Android phone that receives the supported wallet messages, then pair it with your workspace.
  4. Test the available dashboard features during your 7-day free trial. No payment is required to start.
  5. After the trial, contact MalawiHub for manual activation. Payment can be made in person using cash, Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba.
After the trial: Paid access is activated manually after payment is confirmed and lasts for 30 days. It is not an automatic mobile-money checkout.
