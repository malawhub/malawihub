🔐 Private workspace

How to add an employee
1. Back up your private key.
2. Generate an employee invitation link.
3. Copy the link and send it privately to your employee.
4. Your employee opens the link, signs in or creates an account, enters their name, and taps Join Business.

Your private key stays in this browser. Back it up before changing phones.
