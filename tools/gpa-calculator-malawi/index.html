GPA Calculator for University Students in Malawi

Use MalawiHub to calculate your GPA from courses, credit values and grades. The calculator uses the grading-scale data available for each selected university and does not guess a conversion when reliable data is unavailable.

Open GPA Calculator

How to calculate your GPA

  1. Select your university.
  2. Enter each course and its credit value.
  3. Select the grade for each course.
  4. Calculate your GPA.

Why use a university-specific GPA calculator?

Universities can use different grading scales. Selecting the appropriate scale helps avoid applying the wrong grade-point conversion. Always compare the result with your university's official academic regulations.
