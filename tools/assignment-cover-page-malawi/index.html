How to make an assignment cover page

  1. Open the MalawiHub assignment cover page generator.
  2. Select your university or college.
  3. Enter your school, student name and registration number.
  4. Add your department, course, course code and assignment title.
  5. Enter your lecturer and submission date.
  6. Select Generate Cover Page and use the PDF/print option.

What information should an assignment cover page contain?

A typical university or college assignment cover page identifies the institution, school or faculty, department, course, assignment title or number, student name, student identification number, lecturer or instructor and submission date. Always follow your institution's own submission requirements if they differ.
