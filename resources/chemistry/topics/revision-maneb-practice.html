2. Essential Formulae

moles: n = m/M

concentration: c = n/V

gas volume: n = V/24

heat energy: q = mcΔT

4. MANEB-style Practice

1. An atom has atomic number 17 and mass number 35. Find its number of neutrons.

2. Balance: Fe + O₂ → Fe₂O₃.

3. Calculate the moles in 18 g of H₂O. M = 18 g mol⁻¹.

4. Write the formula of calcium nitrate.

5. State two factors affecting reaction rate.

6. What gas turns limewater milky?

7. What happens at the cathode during electrolysis?

8. Give two effects of acid rain.

9. Why does graphite conduct electricity?

10. What is the purpose of a catalyst?

Answers

  1. 35 − 17 = 18 neutrons.
  2. 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃.
  3. n = 18/18 = 1 mol.
  4. Ca(NO₃)₂.
  5. Temperature and concentration. Surface area and catalysts are also correct.
  6. Carbon dioxide.
  7. Reduction occurs; positive ions gain electrons.
  8. Examples: damage to vegetation and acidification of water.
  9. It has mobile/delocalised electrons.
  10. To change reaction rate without being permanently consumed.

5. Practical Examination Focus

  • Read measuring instruments at eye level.
  • Record observations accurately.
  • Include units in calculations.
  • Repeat measurements where necessary.
  • Identify anomalous results.
  • Distinguish observations from conclusions.
  • Know common gas and ion tests.

6. Common Mistakes

  • Unbalanced equations.
  • Wrong units.
  • Incorrect mole ratios.
  • Confusing cathode and anode.
  • Confusing observation with conclusion.
  • Giving an explanation without the scientific reason.

7. Examination Tips

  1. Read the question carefully.
  2. Show calculation steps.
  3. Write balanced equations.
  4. Check units.
  5. Explain answers when asked to explain.
  6. Check your work before submitting.

8. Final Revision Rule

Understand → Practise → Mark → Correct → Practise again.
