📝 University Chemistry Revision & Practice

A university-focused revision area for reviewing concepts, calculations and practical Chemistry.

How to use this section

1. Review the concept

Read the topic notes and identify definitions, principles, equations and assumptions.

2. Work through examples

Follow each calculation or reaction step and check units and significant figures.

3. Practise without notes

Attempt questions first, then compare your method with the worked solution.

4. Record weak areas

Return to topics where you make repeated errors and practise them again.

🧪 Chemistry areas to revise

🔢 Calculation checklist

  • Amount of substance and molar mass
  • Concentration and dilution
  • Stoichiometric relationships
  • Percentage yield and purity
  • Gas calculations
  • Energy and thermochemistry calculations
  • Rate and kinetic calculations
  • Electrochemical calculations
  • Analytical results, uncertainty and significant figures
