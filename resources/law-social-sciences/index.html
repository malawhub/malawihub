⚖️ Law & Social Sciences

General academic study areas for Law, History, Sociology, Political Science, Psychology and related disciplines.

Law

Legal reasoning, case analysis, legal research, statutes, contracts and constitutional concepts. Always verify current law from authoritative sources.

History

Historical methods, source criticism, historiography, chronology and evidence-based argument.

Sociology

Social institutions, culture, inequality, socialisation, research methods and social change.

Political Science

Institutions, political systems, public policy, governance and comparative analysis.

Psychology

Major psychological approaches, cognition, development, social behaviour and research methods.

Geography

Physical and human geography, spatial analysis, environment, population and development.

Communication

Academic communication, media, language, argumentation and information literacy.

Academic Argument

Build claims from evidence, compare sources, acknowledge limitations and reference correctly.
