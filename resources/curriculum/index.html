📚 Form 1–4 Learning Hub

Progressive secondary-school study organization for MalawiHub. Each form is separated so students can study at the appropriate level.

How to use: Start with your Form, choose a subject, then study the listed topics. Topic pages are being expanded with explanations, worked examples, examination practice and labelled diagrams where a visual explanation helps.

Form 1 — Foundation

🧪 Chemistry

Matter, particles, atomic structure, formulae, equations, separation and introductory reactions.

Study Chemistry →

🧮 Mathematics

Number, arithmetic, algebraic foundations, geometry, measurement and introductory statistics.

Study Mathematics →

⚛️ Physics

Measurement, forces, motion, matter, energy, heat, light and introductory electricity.

Study Physics →

🧬 Biology

Living organisms, cells, nutrition, classification, health and basic ecology.

Study Biology →

Form 2 — Core Development

🧪 Chemistry

Periodic trends, bonding, mole calculations, acids/bases, metals, energy and rates.

Study Chemistry →

🧮 Mathematics

Algebra, equations, graphs, geometry, trigonometry foundations, statistics and probability.

Study Mathematics →

⚛️ Physics

Motion, pressure, thermal physics, waves, electricity, magnetism and applications.

Study Physics →

🧬 Biology

Plant and animal systems, reproduction, transport, respiration, ecology and health.

Study Biology →

Form 3 — Advanced Concepts

🧪 Chemistry

Organic chemistry, electrochemistry, qualitative analysis, advanced calculations and practical chemistry.

Study Chemistry →

🧮 Mathematics

Functions, advanced algebra, trigonometry, vectors, matrices, statistics and introductory calculus.

Study Mathematics →

⚛️ Physics

Advanced mechanics, electricity, waves, optics, thermal physics and quantitative problem solving.

Study Physics →

🧬 Biology

Genetics, evolution, physiology, microbiology, ecology and advanced biological processes.

Study Biology →

Form 4 — Consolidation & MSCE Preparation

🧪 Chemistry

Consolidation of organic/inorganic/physical chemistry, practical skills and examination-style questions.

Revise Chemistry →

🧮 Mathematics

Revise Mathematics →

⚛️ Physics

Consolidation, formulas, calculations, practical interpretation and examination practice.

Revise Physics →

🧬 Biology

Revise Biology →
