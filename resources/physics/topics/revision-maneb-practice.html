2. Quick Revision

  • Measurement: SI units, accuracy, precision and errors.
  • Motion: distance, displacement, speed, velocity and acceleration.
  • Forces: Newton's laws, weight, friction and equilibrium.
  • Energy: work, kinetic energy, potential energy, power and efficiency.
  • Thermal physics: heat, temperature, expansion and heat transfer.
  • Waves: wavelength, frequency, amplitude and wave speed.
  • Light: reflection, refraction, lenses and total internal reflection.
  • Electricity: current, voltage, resistance, circuits and power.
  • Magnetism: magnetic fields, electromagnets and motors.
  • Induction: generators, transformers and electromagnetic induction.
  • Nuclear physics: isotopes, radiation, half-life, fission and fusion.
  • Energy resources: solar, hydro, wind, biomass and other renewable sources.
  • Practical work: variables, tables, graphs, errors and conclusions.

3. MANEB-Style Practice

1. A car travels 240 m in 20 s. Find its speed.
2. A 4 kg object accelerates at 5 m/s². Find the force.
3. Find the weight of a 10 kg object if \(g=10\text{ N/kg}\).
4. Find the work done by a 30 N force moving an object 4 m.
5. Find the kinetic energy of a 2 kg object moving at 6 m/s.
6. Find the density of a 500 g object with volume 100 cm³.
7. Find the pressure produced by 100 N over 0.5 m².
8. Find the moment of 20 N acting 0.3 m from a pivot.
9. A wave has \(f=50\) Hz and \(\lambda=4\) m. Find its speed.
10. Find current when 60 C flows in 20 s.
11. Find voltage when \(I=3\) A and \(R=4\Omega\).
12. Find power of an appliance using 240 V and 2 A.
13. State Newton's first law.
14. State two laws of reflection.
15. What determines the pitch of sound?
16. State two factors affecting electromagnet strength.
17. Define electromagnetic induction.
18. Define half-life.
19. Name the three types of nuclear radiation.
20. Give two renewable energy sources.
21. What is an independent variable?
22. State two rules for drawing graphs.
23. State one source of random error.
24. Why are gaps left between railway tracks?
25. Why does a sharp knife produce greater pressure?
26. State one use of ultrasound.
27. State one use of a transformer.
28. State one use of radioactivity.
29. State one difference between series and parallel circuits.
30. State two practical safety precautions.

14. Angle of incidence equals angle of reflection; the rays and normal lie in one plane.
15. Frequency.
16. Current and number of turns of the coil.
17. Production of e.m.f. due to changing magnetic flux.
18. Time required for activity or undecayed nuclei to fall to half its original value.
19. Alpha, beta and gamma.
20. Solar and hydroelectric energy.
21. The variable deliberately changed.
22. Label axes with units; use a suitable scale.
23. Reaction-time variation.
24. To allow thermal expansion.
25. Its smaller area produces greater pressure for the same force.
26. Medical imaging.
27. To step voltage up or down.
28. Cancer treatment or medical diagnosis.
29. Series has one main path; parallel has separate branches.
30. Keep electrical equipment dry; do not touch exposed live conductors.

5. Final Exam Tips

  • Write the formula before calculating.
  • Show your working.
  • Always include units.
  • Label diagrams clearly.
  • Read graphs carefully.
  • For practical questions, identify variables and errors.
  • Check your final answer before moving on.
