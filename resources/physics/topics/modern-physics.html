Modern Physics

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Special relativity changes how space and time are related at high speeds.
  • Quantum theory describes energy and matter at microscopic scales.
  • Atomic spectra arise from quantised energy levels.
  • Nuclear processes involve binding energy and radioactive decay.
  • Models should be used within the assumptions under which they are valid.

Practice checklist

  • Keep units consistent in energy calculations.
  • Distinguish wavelength, frequency and photon energy.
  • Write radioactive decay equations carefully.
  • State assumptions when applying a model.
