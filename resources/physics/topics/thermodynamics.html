Thermodynamics

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Temperature describes thermal state while heat is energy transferred because of a temperature difference.
  • The first law relates heat, work and internal-energy change.
  • The second law introduces entropy and direction of spontaneous processes.
  • State functions depend on state rather than path.
  • Thermodynamic cycles can be analysed from energy transfers.

Practice checklist

  • Define the system and surroundings.
  • Use a consistent sign convention for work and heat.
  • Distinguish reversible and irreversible processes.
  • Check whether the final state is physically reasonable.
