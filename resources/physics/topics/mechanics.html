Mechanics

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Kinematics describes motion using displacement, velocity and acceleration.
  • Newton's laws relate forces to motion.
  • Work transfers energy through a force acting over displacement.
  • Momentum is conserved in an isolated system.
  • Angular motion uses rotational analogues of linear quantities.

Practice checklist

  • Draw a free-body diagram.
  • Choose a sign convention and keep it throughout.
  • Write the governing equation before substituting numbers.
  • Check units and limiting behaviour.
