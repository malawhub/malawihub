Waves & Optics

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Wave speed relates frequency and wavelength by v = fλ.
  • Interference results from superposition of waves.
  • Diffraction becomes important when an aperture or obstacle is comparable with wavelength.
  • Refraction changes wave direction when speed changes between media.
  • Polarisation describes the orientation of transverse wave oscillations.

Practice checklist

  • Identify the medium and wavelength before calculating.
  • Distinguish constructive from destructive interference.
  • Use a labelled ray diagram for refraction problems.
  • Check whether angles are measured from the normal.
