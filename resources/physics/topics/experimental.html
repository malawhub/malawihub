UNIVERSITY STUDY GUIDE

Experimental Physics

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Every measurement has uncertainty.
  • Random and systematic errors have different causes and treatments.
  • Graphs should show variables, units and meaningful scales.
  • Calibration compares an instrument response with a known standard.
  • A scientific report connects observations, calculations and conclusions.

Practice checklist

  • Record raw data with units and sensible significant figures.
  • Estimate uncertainty rather than hiding it.
  • Use a suitable graph and identify trends.
  • Explain major sources of error and how they affect the result.
