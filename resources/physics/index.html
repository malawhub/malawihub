⚛️ University Physics

Core university Physics areas with an emphasis on understanding equations and solving numerical problems.

Mechanics

Motion, forces, work, energy, momentum, rotation, gravitation and oscillations.

Electricity & Magnetism

Electric fields, potential, circuits, capacitance, magnetic fields, induction and electromagnetic applications.

Waves & Optics

Wave motion, interference, diffraction, polarisation, reflection and refraction.

Thermodynamics

Temperature, heat, work, internal energy, laws of thermodynamics and entropy.

Modern Physics

Relativity, quantum ideas, atomic structure, nuclear physics and particle concepts.

Mathematical Physics

Vectors, differential equations, coordinate systems and mathematical tools used in physical models.

Experimental Physics

Measurement, uncertainty, graphs, calibration, error analysis and scientific reporting.

Problem Solving

Draw the physical model, list known values, choose the governing equation, substitute carefully and check units.
