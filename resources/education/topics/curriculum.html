Curriculum Studies

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • A curriculum includes intended learning, content, experiences and assessment.
  • Curriculum development requires clear aims and alignment between teaching and assessment.
  • Implementation is affected by teachers, learners, resources and context.
  • Evaluation examines whether intended outcomes are being achieved.
  • Curriculum change should be based on evidence and stakeholder needs.

Practice checklist

  • Write measurable learning outcomes.
  • Map outcomes to teaching activities and assessment.
  • Distinguish intended from implemented curriculum.
  • Evaluate a curriculum using explicit criteria.
