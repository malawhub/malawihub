Teaching Methods

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Learner-centred teaching actively involves students in constructing and applying knowledge.
  • A lesson plan should connect objectives, activities, resources and assessment.
  • Questioning can check understanding and stimulate reasoning.
  • Differentiation adjusts support, process or output to learner needs.
  • Feedback is most useful when it is specific and actionable.

Practice checklist

  • Write a lesson objective that can be assessed.
  • Choose a method that matches the learning objective.
  • Prepare questions at different cognitive levels.
  • Use a short assessment to check learning before ending the lesson.
