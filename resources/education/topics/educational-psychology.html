UNIVERSITY STUDY GUIDE

Educational Psychology

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Learning theories explain how learners acquire and use knowledge.
  • Motivation can be intrinsic or influenced by external goals and environments.
  • Development affects cognition, language, identity and social behaviour.
  • Individual differences should be considered without assuming one method works for everyone.
  • Assessment evidence can inform teaching decisions.

Practice checklist

  • Compare major learning theories.
  • Give a classroom example for each concept.
  • Separate observation from interpretation.
  • Connect theory to a specific teaching situation.
