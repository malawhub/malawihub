UNIVERSITY STUDY GUIDE

Genetics

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Genes are DNA sequences that contribute to biological traits through gene products.
  • DNA replication preserves genetic information.
  • Transcription produces RNA from DNA and translation produces proteins from mRNA.
  • Mutations can alter genetic information and their effects vary.
  • Inheritance patterns depend on alleles, chromosomes and reproductive processes.

Practice checklist

  • Solve simple Mendelian crosses.
  • Distinguish genotype from phenotype.
  • Explain replication, transcription and translation.
  • Use a pedigree carefully and state assumptions.
