UNIVERSITY STUDY GUIDE

Biochemistry

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Proteins, carbohydrates, lipids and nucleic acids have characteristic structures and functions.
  • Enzymes increase reaction rates by lowering activation energy.
  • Metabolic pathways transfer matter and energy through linked reactions.
  • ATP couples energy-releasing and energy-requiring processes.
  • pH and temperature can affect protein structure and enzyme activity.

Practice checklist

  • Identify major biomolecule classes.
  • Explain enzyme-substrate specificity.
  • Interpret a simple enzyme-rate graph.
  • Distinguish catabolic and anabolic pathways.
