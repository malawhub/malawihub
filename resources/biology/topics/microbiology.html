Microbiology

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Microorganisms include bacteria, archaea, fungi, protozoa and viruses.
  • Microbial growth depends on nutrients, temperature, pH and other conditions.
  • Aseptic technique reduces contamination.
  • Staining and culture methods help identify microorganisms.
  • Antimicrobial resistance can arise through selection and gene transfer.

Practice checklist

  • Differentiate major microbial groups.
  • Explain a growth curve.
  • Describe why aseptic technique matters.
  • Interpret basic culture observations without overclaiming identification.
