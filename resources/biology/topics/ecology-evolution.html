UNIVERSITY STUDY GUIDE

Ecology & Evolution

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Populations change through births, deaths, immigration and emigration.
  • Energy flows through ecosystems while nutrients cycle.
  • Natural selection acts on heritable variation.
  • Biodiversity is influenced by environmental and evolutionary processes.
  • Evolutionary relationships can be inferred from shared characteristics and molecular evidence.

Practice checklist

  • Calculate simple population measures.
  • Construct a food web and identify trophic levels.
  • Distinguish natural selection from individual adaptation.
  • Interpret evidence for common ancestry.
