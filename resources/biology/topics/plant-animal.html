Plant & Animal Biology

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Plants move water and solutes through specialised tissues.
  • Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy.
  • Animals have specialised tissues and organ systems.
  • Homeostasis maintains internal conditions within suitable ranges.
  • Reproduction transfers genetic information to the next generation.

Practice checklist

  • Relate structure to function.
  • Compare transport systems in plants and animals.
  • Trace a physiological process from stimulus to response.
  • Use labelled diagrams when describing systems.
