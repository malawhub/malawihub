📱 Digital Study

Use phones, computers and online resources effectively to support your learning.

1. Choose Reliable Resources

Use trustworthy textbooks, educational websites, university materials and other reliable learning resources.

2. Organize Your Files

Create folders for different subjects and keep notes, assignments and study materials organized.

3. Reduce Notifications

Turn off unnecessary notifications during study sessions so that messages and apps do not constantly interrupt your concentration.

4. Use Your Phone as a Study Tool

Use calculators, dictionaries, educational apps, note-taking tools and other useful learning applications when appropriate.

5. Search Effectively

Use specific keywords when searching for information online. Check important information against reliable sources.

6. Take Digital Notes

Use a notes application or document editor to record important ideas, questions, formulas and revision points.

7. Use Digital Practice

Take online quizzes, practise questions and interactive exercises to test your understanding.

8. Avoid Digital Distractions

Social media, games and entertainment can consume study time. Use app limits or focus modes when necessary.

9. Protect Your Work

Back up important assignments, notes and documents so that you do not lose your work if your device develops a problem.

Simple Digital Study Routine

Choose a resource → Study → Take notes → Practise → Save your work → Review.
