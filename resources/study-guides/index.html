📊 University Study Guides

Practical academic skills that work across university subjects.

Effective Revision

Use active recall, spaced review, practice questions and error correction rather than only rereading notes.

Exam Preparation

Plan revision around the syllabus or course outline, past questions, weak areas and timed practice.

Open Exam Preparation

Academic Writing

Plan arguments, use evidence, structure paragraphs and reference sources consistently.

Research Skills

Develop research questions, search literature, evaluate sources, collect data and report findings.

Laboratory Reports

Present aims, methods, observations, results, calculations, discussion, conclusions and references clearly.

Time Management

Break large assignments into small tasks and schedule study around lectures, practicals and deadlines.

Academic Integrity

Understand plagiarism, paraphrasing, quotation, referencing and responsible use of study tools.

Study with Low Data

Prefer lightweight pages, read online before downloading large files and save only resources you actually need.
