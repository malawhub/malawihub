📅 Revision Planning

Learn how to organize your revision and make the most of your available study time.

1. List Your Topics

Write down all the subjects and topics you need to revise. This gives you a clear picture of what needs to be covered.

2. Set Your Priorities

Give more attention to difficult topics, important areas and subjects where your understanding is weak.

3. Make a Revision Calendar

Assign specific topics to specific days. Spread revision across several days instead of trying to cover everything at once.

4. Use Short Study Sessions

Break long study periods into focused sessions with suitable short breaks.

5. Mix Different Subjects

Where practical, alternate between subjects or different types of problems to keep your revision varied.

6. Include Practice Questions

Do not spend all your revision time reading. Include questions and exercises to check what you can actually recall and apply.

7. Track Your Progress

Mark topics as completed and record areas that still need more attention.

8. Leave Time for Review

Keep some time near the end of your plan for reviewing difficult topics and correcting mistakes.

9. Adjust Your Plan

If a topic takes longer than expected, adjust the timetable instead of abandoning the entire revision plan.

Simple Revision Cycle

Plan → Study → Recall → Practise → Check → Review → Repeat.
