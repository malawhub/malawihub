📝 Note-Taking

Learn how to create clear, useful notes that make studying and revision easier.

1. Focus on Key Ideas

Do not try to write every word. Identify the main ideas, important facts, definitions, formulas and examples.

2. Use Your Own Words

Rewrite information in language you understand. This can help you process the material instead of simply copying it.

3. Use Headings and Subheadings

Organize your notes into sections. Clear headings make it easier to find information during revision.

4. Use Short Points

Use short sentences, keywords and bullet points instead of long paragraphs whenever possible.

5. Record Important Examples

Include useful examples, calculations and worked solutions that help explain difficult concepts.

6. Use Diagrams

For subjects such as Biology, Chemistry and Geography, diagrams, tables and flowcharts can make relationships easier to understand.

7. Highlight Important Information

Mark important definitions, formulas and key points, but avoid highlighting almost everything.

8. Review Your Notes

Read and improve your notes after class. Fill in missing information while the lesson is still fresh in your mind.

9. Turn Notes Into Questions

Convert important points into questions and later try to answer them without looking at your notes.

Simple Note-Taking Formula

Listen → Identify key ideas → Write briefly → Organize → Review → Test yourself.
