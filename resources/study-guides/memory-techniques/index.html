🧠 Memory Techniques

Simple techniques that can help you remember and recall what you study.

1. Active Recall

Close your notes and try to remember the information from memory. Ask yourself questions and answer without looking at the answers.

2. Spaced Revision

Review information several times over different days instead of trying to memorize everything in one sitting.

3. Use Mnemonics

Create a memorable word, phrase or sentence to help you remember a list or sequence of information.

4. Connect New Ideas

Link new information to something you already know. Meaningful connections can make information easier to remember.

5. Explain It in Your Own Words

Explain a concept as if you were teaching another student. If you cannot explain it clearly, review the difficult part again.

6. Use Practice Questions

Answer questions regularly. Practice helps you remember information and identify areas that need more revision.

7. Make Short Notes

Reduce long material into important points, keywords, formulas and examples. Avoid copying entire textbooks.

8. Use Visual Memory

Use diagrams, charts, tables and simple drawings when they help you understand relationships between ideas.

9. Test Yourself

Before an examination, test yourself without looking at your notes. This shows what you actually remember.

Quick Memory Routine

Learn → Close your notes → Recall → Check → Correct → Review later.
