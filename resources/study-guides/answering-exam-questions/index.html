✍️ Answering Exam Questions

Learn how to understand questions, organize answers and manage your examination time.

1. Read the Question Carefully

Read the entire question before answering. Make sure you understand exactly what is being asked.

2. Identify the Command Word

Look for words such as define, describe, explain, compare, discuss, calculate, identify and evaluate. They tell you what type of answer is required.

3. Highlight Key Information

Identify important words, figures, units, conditions and instructions in the question.

4. Plan Longer Answers

For essays and extended responses, briefly organize your main points before you start writing.

5. Answer Directly

Give an answer that addresses the question. Avoid adding large amounts of unrelated information.

6. Show Your Working

For calculations, show the formula, substitution, working steps and final answer with the correct unit where appropriate.

7. Use Examples When Helpful

Examples can make explanations clearer, especially when a question asks you to explain or illustrate a concept.

8. Manage Your Time

Do not spend too much time on one question. Allocate your time according to the marks and leave time to review your answers.

9. Check Your Answers

Before submitting, check calculations, units, spelling, question numbers and whether every required question has been answered.

Quick Exam Method

Read → Understand → Plan → Answer → Check.
