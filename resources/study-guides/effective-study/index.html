📖 Effective Study Techniques

Practical methods for understanding, remembering and applying what you study.

1. Active Recall

Instead of repeatedly reading your notes, close the book and try to remember the information yourself.

Example: Read a Chemistry topic, close your notes, then write down everything you remember.

2. Spaced Practice

Review information several times over different days instead of trying to learn everything in one sitting.

Example: Review a topic today, again tomorrow, then again after several days.

3. Practice Questions

Test yourself using exercises, past papers and questions similar to those you expect in an examination.

4. Explain What You Learn

Try explaining a difficult concept using your own words. If you cannot explain it clearly, identify the part you need to study again.

5. Make Short Notes

Turn long material into concise notes containing important definitions, formulas, examples and key ideas.

6. Use a Study Timetable

Allocate specific periods for different subjects and include regular breaks.

7. Mix Your Practice

Study different types of questions and related topics rather than practising exactly the same type of problem repeatedly.

8. Review Your Mistakes

Do not simply check whether an answer is wrong. Find out why it was wrong and practise the concept again.

Quick Study Routine

Try this simple routine:

📚 Learn → 🧠 Recall → ✍️ Practise → 🔎 Check → 🔁 Review
