🩺 Health Sciences

General study areas for students in health-related university programmes.

Study aid only: these resources support academic learning and are not a substitute for clinical supervision, professional guidance or current clinical protocols.

Anatomy

Human structure, anatomical terminology, tissues, organs and body systems.

Physiology

Normal functions of cells, tissues, organs and integrated body systems.

Biochemistry

Biomolecules, enzymes, metabolism and biochemical regulation.

Microbiology

Microorganisms, infection concepts, laboratory identification and prevention.

Pharmacology

Drug classes, mechanisms, pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics as academic concepts.

Pathology

General mechanisms of disease, cellular injury, inflammation and major pathological processes.

Public Health

Epidemiology, health promotion, prevention, population health and health systems.

Research & Evidence

Study design, literature searching, critical appraisal, data interpretation and academic writing.
