Numerical Methods

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Numerical methods approximate solutions when exact algebraic solutions are difficult or unavailable.
  • Bisection brackets a root and repeatedly halves the interval.
  • Newton's method uses the derivative to improve a root estimate.
  • Numerical integration approximates area from function values.
  • Error should be estimated and reported with appropriate precision.

Practice checklist

  • State the numerical method before calculating.
  • Choose a sensible initial interval or starting value.
  • Show iterations clearly.
  • Compare the approximation with a known or independently checked value.
