Mathematical Methods

Use this page to review the core ideas, then practise with your lecture questions, tutorials and past papers.

Core ideas

  • Sequences and series require attention to convergence.
  • Partial derivatives measure change with respect to one variable while others are held constant.
  • Multiple integrals extend integration to areas and volumes.
  • Vectors and coordinate systems are common in science and engineering.
  • Dimensional consistency helps detect mistakes in applied mathematics.

Practice checklist

  • Test whether a series converges before using it.
  • Keep variables and constants clearly separated.
  • Sketch regions before setting up multiple integrals.
  • Check dimensions in applied formulas.
