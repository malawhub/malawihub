Problem Solving

Translate the question into mathematics, identify the method, show working, check units and verify the answer.

📌 University Mathematics Checklist

Before solving: write what is known and what is required.

During solving: show every important algebraic step and keep notation consistent.

After solving: check the sign, units, domain and whether the answer is reasonable.
