How to create your lesson

  1. Enter your lesson title, subject and class.
  2. Add scenes. Each scene becomes one slide in your video.
  3. Write a short heading, explanation and key points.
  4. Open Your voice and record your narration or upload an audio file.
  5. Add your own image or diagram in Visuals.
  6. Preview the lesson, then render and download your video.

Microphone recording requires a secure website (HTTPS) and browser permission. Video export support depends on your browser. Chrome on Android is recommended. Exported video is WebM; convert it to MP4 with a trusted video editor if your platform requires MP4.
