MalawiHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Applies to: MalawiHub Business Connector and the related MalawiHub Business Hub services.

1. What the Business Connector does

MalawiHub Business Connector is a business-phone companion application. With the business user's permission, it receives supported transaction SMS notifications from the connected phone and extracts transaction information needed for business wallet monitoring.

2. SMS information

The application requests SMS access because receiving supported transaction messages is a core function of the Business Connector. It processes supported Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba transaction messages and sends relevant transaction information to the user's authorized MalawiHub Business Hub workspace.

The connector is designed to process supported business transaction messages rather than provide general SMS management. Unrecognized messages are ignored.

3. Information processed

  • Transaction provider (for example Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba).
  • Transaction type, amount, balance and transaction reference when present.
  • Transaction time and a transaction summary.
  • A cryptographic hash used to prevent duplicate transaction ingestion.
  • Device connection and pairing information required to authenticate the connected business phone.

4. How information is used

The information is used to provide business wallet monitoring, balances, transaction messages and related features inside the authorized MalawiHub Business Hub workspace. It is not used for advertising or selling SMS data.

5. Sharing and storage

Transaction information is transmitted securely to MalawiHub's backend services and is made available only to authorized Business Hub users according to the business workspace permissions. The connector does not intentionally publish private SMS information publicly.

6. User control

The business phone owner controls whether the application is installed and whether SMS permissions are granted. Removing SMS permissions stops the connector from receiving transaction SMS notifications. Disconnecting the device stops the device from sending new connector events to the Business Hub.

7. Security

Device pairing uses a temporary pairing code and an authenticated device token. The connector uses HTTPS for communication with the MalawiHub backend.

8. Children's privacy

The Business Connector is a business utility and is not directed to children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's data practices or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning the Business Connector, use the contact and administrator channels provided inside the MalawiHub Business Hub.
